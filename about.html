ABOUT HIWIN

Driving industrial automation forward with precision motion technology.

Our Mission

At HIWIN, our mission is to provide high-quality precision motion control products for the industrial automation sector. We are dedicated to delivering innovative solutions that enhance manufacturing efficiency and product reliability.

Our Vision

We strive to be the global leader in motion control technology, offering an extensive range of products including linear guideways, ball screws, servo motors, and integrated automation systems.

Quality Assurance

Quality is at the core of everything we do. Our state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities and rigorous testing procedures ensure that every product meets the highest industry standards for accuracy, durability, and performance.

HIWIN Industrial Automation delivers precision motion systems and industrial components for manufacturing excellence.
